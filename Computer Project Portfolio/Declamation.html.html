What is a Declamation?

Declamation is about building your self-confidence, public speaking, and memorization skills. In some declamation performances or programs, speeches are created based what topic you're assigned and you have to memorize it.

Creating a Speech

In creating a speech, you're given a specific topic to talk about and you must memorize it at an exact time. For example, the topic I got was Discrimination and talked about Bullying, because it's a long-time issue that has been going around for centuries.
