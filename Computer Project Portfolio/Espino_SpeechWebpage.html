About my Declamation Speech

Hi everyone, I’m Judd Cedric L. Espino, and I want to talk about how being too kind and friendly can sometimes lead to bullying. I've always believed in being kind, helping others, being there for friends but sometimes people take that kindness the other way. They ask for too much, blame you for things, or assume you’re okay with everything. I’ve stayed quiet at times, not wanting to hurt others' feelings, but it left me feeling used and unseen. When people see kindness as weakness, they take advantage of it. It’s a form of bullying that’s often overlooked but can be just as hurtful. Cyrus the Great, the Persian King, believed in fairness and respect, and his kindness came with strength. I’m learning that being kind doesn’t mean saying yes to everything; it’s about helping others while also valuing yourself. To those who feel this way: your kindness is a strength, not something to be abused. Set boundaries and stand up for yourself. And to everyone else, don’t take the kind people for granted; they deserve respect too.
